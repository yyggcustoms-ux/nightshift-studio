Creating a Post-Holiday Season Career Strategy

As the holiday season comes to a close, many individuals are reflecting on their career goals and seeking to make a change or improvement in the new year. With the start of a new year, it's an ideal time to set new year career goals and create a strategy to achieve them. The post-holiday season is a great opportunity to reassess priorities, update professional materials, and make a plan to attract new career opportunities.

Setting the Foundation

To create a successful career strategy, it's essential to start by reflecting on past experiences, skills, and achievements. Take time to think about what worked well in the previous year and what areas need improvement. Consider what motivates and fulfills you in your career, and what kind of work environment and culture you thrive in.

Identifying Career Objectives

Identifying clear career objectives is crucial to creating a effective strategy. Ask yourself:

  • What are my short-term and long-term career goals?
  • What skills do I need to acquire or improve to achieve my goals?
  • What kind of industry or work environment do I want to be in?
  • What are my non-negotiables in a career?

Building a Strong Online Presence

Having a strong online presence is vital in today's digital age. Here are some steps to build a professional online presence:

  • Create a professional LinkedIn profile, including a clear and concise headline, summary, and experience section
  • Ensure consistency across all social media platforms, including Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram
  • Develop a personal website or blog to showcase skills, experience, and achievements
  • Engage with industry leaders and like-minded professionals on social media to build connections and stay up-to-date on industry news

Updating Your Resume

A well-written and tailored resume is essential to attracting new career opportunities. Here are some steps to update your resume:

  • Review and update your work experience, skills, and education sections
  • Tailor your resume to your target industry and job title
  • Use clear and concise language, and bullet points to make your resume easy to scan
  • Include relevant keywords and phrases from the job description to pass through applicant tracking systems (ATS)

Creating a Career Action Plan

To achieve your career goals, it's essential to create a career action plan. Here's an example template:

Career Action Plan Template

| Goal | Action Steps | Timeline | Resources |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Update LinkedIn profile | Research and write a new summary, update experience section, and add new connections | 1 week | LinkedIn guide, career coach |

| Improve public speaking skills | Take an online course, practice with friends and family, and join a public speaking group | 3 months | Online course, public speaking group |

| Network with industry leaders | Research and reach out to 10 industry leaders, attend 2 industry events, and engage with industry leaders on social media | 2 months | Industry events, social media |

Staying Focused and Motivated

Staying focused and motivated is crucial to achieving your career goals. Here are some tips:

  • Break down large goals into smaller, manageable tasks
  • Create a schedule and stick to it
  • Celebrate small wins and accomplishments
  • Surround yourself with supportive people who encourage and motivate you

Achieving New Year Career Goals

To achieve your new year career goals, it's essential to stay focused, motivated, and committed to your career action plan. Remember to regularly review and update your plan, and make adjustments as needed. With a clear plan and a strong online presence, you'll be well on your way to attracting new career opportunities and achieving your goals.
